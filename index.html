Generative engine optimisation (GEO) toolkit

AI Answer Share Lab

When buyers ask an AI assistant “what’s the best tool for…”, does your brand get named, and in what position? Paste the answers you collected and see share of voice, the prompts you’re missing, and the sources the engines lean on. Everything runs in your browser.

How it works

1. Brands

One brand per line: Name | alias one, alias two | own. Mark exactly one as own. Matching is whole-word and case-insensitive.

2. Prompt panel optional

Generate a buying-journey prompt set, run each prompt in the assistants you track, and paste the answers back as CSV.

    3. Answers

    CSV columns: prompt, answer (required) plus optional engine, intent, snapshot. Use two or more snapshot dates to see the trend. Aliases like query, response, model, date also work.

    Plain-English readout

      Brand scorecard

      Position-weighted share of voice

      Each answer gives the first brand named 1 point, the second ½, the third ⅓, and so on. Share = a brand’s points ÷ all points.

      By engine

      Mention rate per assistant.

      By buying stage

      Mention rate per prompt intent.

      Trend between snapshots

      Prompt gaps

      Prompts where competitors are named but your brand is missing in at least one engine, ranked by how many engines miss you × how many rivals appear.

      Sources the engines cite

      Domains found in answers. A source that is mostly cited in answers without your brand is a PR, review-site or listing opportunity.

      Answer explorer

      How it works

      • Mention rate: share of answers that name the brand (or an alias), with a 95% Wilson interval so small samples aren’t over-read.
      • First-place rate and average rank: the order brands are first named in each answer.
      • Share of voice: plain (mentions ÷ all brand mentions) and position-weighted (1, ½, ⅓…).
      • Tone: a simple positive/negative word list applied to sentences that name the brand. It is a rough signal, not a sentiment model.
      • Trend: mention rate between the last two snapshots with a two-proportion z-test.
      • Limits: AI answers vary run to run. Run each prompt more than once per engine, keep the panel fixed between snapshots, and treat small differences with care.
